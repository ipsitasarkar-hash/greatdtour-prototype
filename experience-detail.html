Day-by-Day Itinerary

1
Arrival in Manali
Welcome dinner and trip orientation. Meet your group and guides over a traditional Himachali meal. Evening briefing on safety protocols and trek overview.
2
Acclimatization & Yoga
Light hiking and evening yoga session. Introduction to pranayama breathing techniques to aid altitude acclimatization. Gentle 3 km nature walk through apple orchards.
3
Trek to Base Camp
5-hour trek through pine forests. Evening meditation session under the stars at 3,200m elevation. Overnight in tented camps with hot meals provided.
4
Mountain Exploration
Sunrise yoga and local village visit. Cultural immersion with the Gaddi shepherd community — learn about their ancient migratory traditions and nomadic lifestyle.
5
Summit Day
Early morning summit attempt and celebration. Sunrise yoga at the peak with panoramic 360° Himalayan views. This is the highlight of the trek — prepare for an unforgettable sunrise.
6
Descent & Relaxation
Return trek and restorative yoga. Hot spring visit in the evening for well-deserved relaxation. Overnight in a comfortable mountain guesthouse with hot showers.
7
Cultural Immersion
Visit to local monastery and meditation. Traditional weaving and craft workshop with local artisans. Farewell group dinner with cultural folk performances.
8
Departure
Farewell breakfast and transfer to Manali. Group photo session and certificates of completion. Drop-off at Manali bus stand or airport by 10 AM.

Booking & Payments
How do I confirm my booking?
Pay a 30% advance online to hold your slot. You'll receive a booking confirmation email within 24 hours. The remaining 70% is due 60 days before the trip start date. For bookings made less than 60 days in advance, full payment is required upfront.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and no-cost EMI on select banks. All transactions are secured and encrypted.
Is GST included in the displayed price?
The displayed price (₹31,500 per person) is exclusive of GST. A 5% GST is applicable on the total amount and will be shown at checkout. A GST invoice will be provided for all bookings.
Trip Logistics
Where does the trip start and end?
The trip starts and ends in Manali, Himachal Pradesh. We provide complimentary transfers from Manali bus stand and Bhuntar Airport. Travel to and from Manali is not included in the package cost.
What is the group size?
We cap each batch at 12 travellers to maintain the quality of experience and ensure personalised attention. A minimum of 4 confirmed participants is required for a batch to depart. If your batch doesn't meet the minimum, you'll be offered an alternative date or a full refund.
What's the accommodation like on the trek?
Nights 1 and 2 are in a comfortable hotel in Manali with en-suite bathrooms. Nights 3–6 are in insulated high-altitude tents (twin-sharing) with sleeping bags and mats provided. Night 7 is in a guesthouse near Manali with hot showers. All accommodation is twin-sharing; single supplements are available on request for ₹4,000 extra.
What kind of food is served during the trek?
All meals are freshly prepared by our trek cook. We serve a mix of North Indian and Himachali cuisine — think hot dal-roti, sabzi, khichdi, and oatmeal for breakfast. We cater to vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dietary needs if notified at booking. Non-vegetarian options are not available on the trek portion.
Health & Safety
How fit do I need to be for this trek?
This is a moderate-difficulty trek. You should be comfortable walking 10–15 km per day with a light daypack and gentle elevation gains. Prior trekking experience is helpful but not essential. We recommend starting brisk walks of 5–8 km/day at least 4 weeks before the trip. No prior yoga experience is needed.
What happens if I experience altitude sickness?
Our guides are trained in wilderness first aid and altitude sickness management. Day 2 is specifically designed for acclimatisation with pranayama breathing. If a participant experiences severe AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness), we will descend to a lower altitude immediately. Our first-aid kit includes altitude medication. Emergency evacuation is possible within 4–6 hours from most points on the route.
Is travel insurance compulsory?
Yes, travel insurance is mandatory for all participants. Your policy must cover medical emergencies, hospitalisation, trip cancellation, and personal liability. We strongly recommend a minimum medical cover of ₹20 lakh for high-altitude trekking. Proof of insurance must be submitted at least 7 days before departure. We can connect you with our preferred insurance partners.
General
Can I book a private/customised trip for my group?
Absolutely. We offer fully private departures for groups of 4 or more. You pick the dates, we plan around you. We can also customise the duration, difficulty level, yoga intensity, and accommodation type. Use the "Request Custom Dates" option in the Availability tab or contact us directly.
What should I pack for this trip?
We'll send you a detailed packing list after booking. Key items: layered warm clothing (it can drop to -5°C at night), waterproof jacket, trekking shoes (ankle support essential), sunscreen SPF 50+, sunglasses, personal medications, a 40L backpack for day hikes, and yoga wear. Sleeping bags and trek poles are provided. Porter services are available to carry your main bag at ₹1,000/day.
Is there mobile network connectivity on the trek?
BSNL has the best coverage in this region — we recommend picking up a local SIM if you need connectivity. Days 3–6 (high-altitude section) will have little to no mobile signal. We consider this part of the experience! Our guides carry a satellite communicator for emergencies.

Still have questions?

Our trip specialists know this route inside out. Drop us a message and we'll get back to you within a few hours.
