Common Questions From NGOs

You'll need: (1) NGO registration certificate or equivalent legal document, (2) a brief description of your organisation's mission and active programs, (3) contact details for your program coordinator, and (4) optional: photos or reports from past volunteer batches. Our team will guide you through the full checklist during onboarding.
We accept wildlife and conservation programs, community development projects, rural education initiatives, research and citizen science programs, environmental restoration drives, women's empowerment projects, and more. If you run a structured, meaningful volunteering program with defined roles and duration, it's likely a good fit.
Volunteers browse your program listing, select a batch and role, and submit an application with their profile and motivation. You receive all applications in your vendor dashboard, where you can review profiles, accept or reject candidates, and send batch confirmation emails — all from one place.
Yes. You can define minimum age, educational background, physical fitness level, required skills, and up to 5 custom screening questions that applicants must answer during the application process. This ensures you receive candidates who are genuinely suitable for your program.
You set the terms. Programs can be fully funded (₹0 fee, stipend offered), partially funded, or cost-recovery (volunteer pays a contribution). The platform supports all models. For funded programs, stipend details are shown prominently on your listing to attract more applicants.
Your dashboard gives you a real-time view of all applications, confirmed batches, volunteer profiles, batch occupancy, and communication history. You can manage multiple programs and batches simultaneously, export volunteer lists, send bulk updates, and generate impact reports for your stakeholders.
