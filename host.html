Why GDT

The Best Experiences Shouldn't Be Hard to Find.

Some of the most thoughtful programmes are built quietly, by people focused on creating great experiences — not promoting them. GDT is built to change that: a single place where independent operators who've given years to what they do can be found by the travellers and volunteers who are actively looking for them.

Intentional Discovery

Every traveller and volunteer on GDT is looking for something specific. You're found because you're the right fit — not because you paid for placement.

Confirmed Bookings, Not Enquiries

GDT is a conversion platform. You receive paid, confirmed bookings. Enquiries, deposits, and abandoned leads are not our model.

Merit Over Money

No promoted slots, no pay-to-be-seen. Every host is verified before going live. After that, reviews and quality do the ranking.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

GDT is a travel-tech conversion platform, not a lead generator. When a traveller books your experience through GDT, you receive a confirmed, paid booking. The platform handles booking confirmation, payment collection, cancellation processing, communications, and review collection. You focus on delivering the experience. Unlike OTAs or aggregators that list thousands of undifferentiated options, GDT limits listings to a small, curated group per experience category. Your listing is not competing against hundreds of similar operators. GDT earns only on completed bookings.
GDT is built for operators who offer genuine multi-day or immersive experiences in Adventure, Wellness, Cultural Immersion, and Wildlife & Nature. This includes established operators, independent guides, wellness practitioners, retreat owners, craft artisans, life coaches, creators, naturalists, cultural curators, and expedition leaders. Scale is not the criterion — the quality of what you have built and your ability to deliver it independently is what matters. GDT does not work with generic sightseeing tours, transport-only providers, or drop-in hourly sessions without a packaged experience component.
GDT onboarding is invite-only. There is no walk-in listing. If you believe your experience fits GDT's experience philosophy, express your interest to our onboarding team at onboarding@gdtclub.com. The onboarding team reviews every submission, assesses your fit, experience quality, safety standards, and delivery history, and reaches out with next steps. Operators selected during GDT's current pre-launch phase are eligible for the Founding Member Cohort.
Founding Partners receive long-term benefits not available to later operators. Commission rates are locked at listing level for 24 months. Organic marketing support starts from day one regardless of listing slab. Each founding partner's host story is researched, written, and published through GDT's channels during the pre-launch period. All founding partner benefits are contractual, documented in a plain-language Founding Partner Agreement. Category positions fill on a rolling basis and close when full. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com to know more.
GDT charges a commission on completed bookings only. There is no listing fee, no monthly subscription, and no charge until a booking completes and the traveller has paid. Commission is set at the listing level, not at the vendor level. A vendor with multiple products can have each listing on a different slab, based on that product's margin and how much operational support you want GDT to provide. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com to know more.
Yes, and it is free. Volunteer tourism, environmental conservation programmes, community-led immersions, and CSR activities that involve no transaction can be listed on GDT at zero commission. GDT is committed to supporting sustainable, responsible, and community-centred travel. Write to volunteer@gdtclub.com to know more about volunteering listings.
Every listing on GDT gives you a professional, bookable presence with your full experience description, itinerary, policies, availability calendar, and host identity. Every listing generates a short-link you can share through your own channels so the traveller lands on a complete itinerary and bookable page — not an enquiry form or PDF. GDT also provides a range of operational, marketing, and commercial support that increases with your chosen listing slab. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com to understand the slabs and benefits.
Yes. Non-GST registered operators can list on GDT. GDT understands the realities of operating in India's experiential travel sector where genuine, niche, high-quality operators are often not GST registered, particularly at early stages. Where GST obligations apply to transactions on the platform, GDT covers the compliance requirements on your behalf. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com for details.
Yes, entirely. There is no mandatory inventory commitment on GDT. You control your availability calendar through your vendor dashboard — which dates are open, how many participants you accept per batch, and when a slot closes. You also choose your booking model per listing: instant confirmation or request-to-confirm, where you review each booking request before it is accepted. GDT does not require you to commit dates you cannot service.
No. You set your own pricing. You list your MRP and run discounts at your own discretion. GDT maintains minimum pricing guidelines to protect the integrity of listings and the traveller experience, but within those guidelines, pricing is entirely yours to manage — the same way you would price through your own retail channels.
GDT is built with the Indian travel sector in mind, which means flexibility in how payments are collected. You choose your payment model at each listing level: collect the balance payment directly from the traveller yourself, or enable GDT to collect full payment on your behalf and settle to you according to the agreed payout schedule. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com for questions about payment options.
