Why We Exist

We built this because travel
deserved better.

01

The Problem

Most travel platforms sell you a destination. We think that's the wrong starting point. The best travel memories aren't about where you went — they're about what you did and who you met while you were there.

02

The Vision

"A world where every journey has a story worth telling — and every storyteller has a platform worth building on."
03

The Mission

  • Connect curious travellers with verified, passionate hosts across India
  • Give volunteering its place at the centre of meaningful travel — not as an afterthought
  • Build a platform where honest content and curated experiences coexist

Travellers

Great D'Tour is an experience-first travel marketplace connecting curious travellers with vetted, passionate hosts offering small-group niche experiences across India.
Every experience on GDT is personally reviewed by our team. We assess the host's credentials, the safety and quality of the experience, and whether it meets our authenticity standard.
Cancellation terms vary by experience and are clearly shown before booking. We always display full policies upfront — no hidden conditions.

Hosts

Guides, experts, farmers, folk musicians, chefs, artists — anyone who has built something real and wants to share it with small, curious groups. Apply via the host application form.
We handle marketing, booking, payment collection, and traveller communications. You focus entirely on delivering a great experience.
Hosts receive secure, on-time payouts after each experience. Full payout schedule and commission details are in the Partnership Guide.
