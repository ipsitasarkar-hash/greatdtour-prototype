About Field Notes

Travel decisions deserve better information than what most of the internet offers.

Field Notes is GDT's travel journal. We started it because we noticed something: the best travel writing wasn't trying to sell you anything. It was honest, specific, and actually useful. So that's what we set out to publish — stories and research that help you plan, think, and choose. Whether you book through GDT or not.

No sponsored content. No listicles engineered for clicks. No FOMO bait. Just writing that respects your time and your curiosity.

Field Notes

Field Notes is GDT's travel journal — honest, independent travel writing that helps you plan better trips. No sponsored content, no listicles, no FOMO bait.
We welcome travellers, hosts, guides, and storytellers who have genuine experience to share. Submit a request to write and our editorial team will review your proposal.
Click "Request Invite to Write" above, fill in your details and a brief pitch. Our editorial team reviews all submissions within 5–7 business days.
We cover Adventure, Wellness & Retreat, Cultural Immersion, Wildlife, Reflections, and Immersive experiences — always with honest, firsthand perspectives.

Travellers

Great D'Tour is an experience-first travel marketplace connecting curious travellers with vetted, passionate hosts offering small-group niche experiences across India.
Every experience on GDT is personally reviewed by our team. We assess the host's credentials, the safety and quality of the experience, and whether it meets our authenticity standard.
Cancellation terms vary by experience and are clearly shown before booking. We always display full policies upfront — no hidden conditions.

Hosts

Guides, experts, farmers, folk musicians, chefs, artists — anyone who has built something real and wants to share it with small, curious groups. Apply via the host application form.
We handle marketing, booking, payment collection, and traveller communications. You focus entirely on delivering a great experience.
Hosts receive secure, on-time payouts after each experience. Full payout schedule and commission details are in the Partnership Guide.
