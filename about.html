Our Story

India has never lacked extraordinary experiences. A Himalayan expedition led by someone who's summited a dozen times. A wildlife immersion with a tracker who reads the forest like a page. A craft trail, a wellness retreat, a village that opens its doors.

The problem was never creating them. It was finding them - and trusting them. Travellers pieced together scattered blogs, WhatsApp numbers, and generic packages. And the people who ran these experiences - brilliant at their craft, invisible online - stayed hidden behind agents and word of mouth.

So we built Great D'Tour: a marketplace where these experiences are easy to find, safe to book, and where the people behind them finally get seen.

02

How We're Building It

We make discovery effortless.

Every experience in one place - searchable, comparable, bookable - so travellers stop hunting and start planning.

We put experts in charge.

Guides, hosts, and practitioners run their own storefront, keep their own identity, and reach travellers directly. We run the machinery; they keep the craft.

We build trust into the system.

Every host is verified before they go live. Every review is real. The best experiences rise because travellers say so - not because we decide.

About Great D'Tour

Great D'Tour is India's experience-led travel marketplace. Instead of sorting travel by destination, we build it around passions - adventure, wellness, cultural immersion, wildlife and nature - plus GDT Care, our volunteer travel programme. Every experience is led by an independent expert who's spent years mastering what they do, and every one is designed to be discovered, compared, and booked in one trusted place.
Booking engines sell you rooms, tickets, and packages. We connect you directly with the people who run the experiences - the guide, the practitioner, the host, the naturalist. No white-labelling, no middleman repackaging someone else's work. You always know exactly who you're travelling with, and what's included, before you book.
Multi-day, immersive, and built around a passion - not a day-tour or a checklist. A Himalayan expedition with someone who's summited a dozen times. A wellness retreat led by a practitioner who trained for years. A craft learned from the family that's kept it alive for generations. If you can find it on a generic booking site, it probably isn't us.
We're just getting started. Field Notes - our editorial space with destination deep-dives, expert wisdom, and traveller stories - is live now and free to explore. GDT Care and our full marketplace are rolling out soon. The best way to be first in line is to join for early access, and we'll bring the experiences to you as they go live.

Travellers

Every host is verified before their experience goes live - we check that they're a legitimate, qualified operator or expert, not an anonymous listing. Beyond that, the marketplace does what marketplaces do best: real reviews from real travellers, so the best hosts rise on merit. We guarantee the floor is safe. Travellers decide what's great.
GDT Care is our volunteer travel programme - for travellers who'd rather contribute than just consume. Across six areas, from nature and conservation to community, craft, and sustainable living, you give your time and skills to the people and places that need them. It's travel that leaves something behind.
When experiences go live, you'll book and pay securely through the platform, with clear pricing and honest cancellation terms shown upfront - no ambiguous PDFs, no advance sent to a stranger's account. Every detail of what's included is on the listing before you commit.

Hosts

We handle marketing, booking, payment collection, and traveller communications. You focus entirely on delivering a great experience.
