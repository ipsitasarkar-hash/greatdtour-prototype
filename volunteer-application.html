Most Popular
🔬 Field Researcher
Wildlife monitoring, camera trap operations, tiger census work, and ecological data collection. Best for science or data-oriented volunteers.
🏫 Community Educator
Education workshops in buffer-zone villages, engaging local families on conservation, human–wildlife coexistence. Best for those with teaching or communication backgrounds.
📷 Documentation Specialist
Photo and video documentation of field activities, habitat restoration, and community work. Your footage is used in advocacy and fundraising. Bring your own camera.

Logistics support, equipment management, volunteer welfare, and daily camp operations. Best for organised, detail-oriented volunteers. Less field time, more systems work.
