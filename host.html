How it works in detail

Every part of the intake, handled.

Getting applications in

One intake for every channel

Share one programme link on Instagram, WhatsApp, your website, college networks and partner groups. Every application arrives in one dashboard, whichever channel it came from.

Screening that works for you

Set eligibility, skills, minimum commitment and screening questions. Upload your own quiz. Applicants who don't meet your criteria are filtered out before you spend a minute on them.

Values and programme fit

Ask what matters to your work: motivation, experience, how they handle community living, what they want from the stay. You'll read answers to the questions you care about, not generic CVs.

Matching and managing

Skills, locations and batches, matched

Running more than one location or batch? Create a programme once, clone it, and adjust each copy. Applicants pick the slot, batch and location that suits them.

Commitment that protects your programme

You set a commitment fee. Applicants pay it when they confirm. If they show up and complete, it's refunded. If they don't show up, it's paid to you.

Fee collection without the back-and-forth

If your programme charges for training, stay or meals, applicants see the full breakdown upfront and pay through the platform on confirmation. No haggling, no chasing.

Staying organised

Your programme in your pocket

Get notified the moment someone applies. Review profiles and accept or decline from the GDT Care Android app, or manage every programme from one desktop dashboard.

Waitlists that keep people engaged

Applicants can see their status at every stage. When a slot opens, your waitlist is ready — and nobody has to chase anybody.

Reviews that build your reputation

Hosts and volunteers review each other after every programme. Strong reviews raise your listing's visibility and help the right applicants find you.

Frequently Asked Questions

GDT is a travel-tech conversion platform, not a lead generator. When a traveller books your experience through GDT, you receive a confirmed, paid booking. The platform handles booking confirmation, payment collection, cancellation processing, communications, and review collection. You focus on delivering the experience. Unlike OTAs or aggregators that list thousands of undifferentiated options, GDT limits listings to a small, curated group per experience category. Your listing is not competing against hundreds of similar operators. GDT earns only on completed bookings.
GDT is built for operators who offer genuine multi-day or immersive experiences in Adventure, Wellness, Cultural Immersion, and Wildlife & Nature. This includes established operators, independent guides, wellness practitioners, retreat owners, craft artisans, life coaches, creators, naturalists, cultural curators, and expedition leaders. Scale is not the criterion, the quality of what you have built and your ability to deliver it independently is what matters. GDT does not work with generic sightseeing tours, transport-only providers, or drop-in hourly sessions without a packaged experience component.
GDT onboarding is invite-only. There is no walk-in listing. If you believe your experience fits GDT's experience philosophy, express your interest to our onboarding team at onboarding@gdtclub.com. The onboarding team reviews every submission, assesses your fit, experience quality, safety standards, and delivery history, and reaches out with next steps. Operators selected during GDT's current pre-launch phase are eligible for the Founding Member Cohort.
Founding Partners receive long-term benefits not available to later operators. Commission rates are locked at listing level for 24 months. Organic marketing support starts from day one regardless of listing slab. Each founding partner's host story is researched, written, and published through GDT's channels during the pre-launch period. All founding partner benefits are contractual, documented in a plain-language Founding Partner Agreement. Category positions fill on a rolling basis and close when full. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com to know more.
GDT charges a commission on completed bookings only. There is no listing fee, no monthly subscription, and no charge until a booking completes and the traveller has paid. Commission is set at the listing level, not at the vendor level. A vendor with multiple products can have each listing on a different slab, based on that product's margin and how much operational support you want GDT to provide. Write to onboarding@gdtclub.com to know more.
