FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before applying.

It's completely free to apply and join. We operate on a commission-only model — you pay nothing upfront. A percentage of each completed booking goes to GDT, which covers platform infrastructure, payment processing, pre-sales support, marketing, and more. No listing fee, no monthly subscriptions.
We welcome adventure treks and expeditions, wellness and yoga retreats, cultural immersion programs, wildlife safaris, culinary trails, craft workshops, heritage walks, and more. If it's an authentic, bookable experience with a genuine host, it likely fits. If you're unsure, apply and we'll let you know.
We review all applications within 5 business days. If you're a good fit, we schedule a 30-minute onboarding call within a week. Most partners complete verification and go live within 3 weeks of their initial application.
For adventure and outdoor experiences, appropriate liability insurance or certifications are required. For cultural, wellness, and culinary experiences, it depends on the nature of the activity. Our onboarding team will walk you through the specific requirements for your category during the verification call.
You set your own prices. Our team provides category benchmarks and demand data to help you price competitively. You can set per-person pricing, group pricing, early-bird discounts, and seasonal rates. Your pricing is always visible to travelers before they book.
Payments are settled within 5–7 business days after the experience is completed. We support bank transfers to any Indian bank account. For GST-registered operators, we can also process full platform settlements. You receive a detailed breakdown for every transaction.
Yes. You can offer both open group experiences (where travelers book individual spots) and private experiences (where a single group books the whole experience). You can also enable custom quote requests for travelers who want bespoke itineraries.
Your dashboard gives you a real-time view of bookings, earnings, upcoming experiences, traveler details, and reviews. You can manage availability, update pricing, respond to queries, and download transaction reports — all in one place.
